## 1. Who They Are

Yousuf Living is a Karachi-based furniture brand that makes upholstered bedroom sets
in its own workshop and sells them directly — no middleman, no showroom markup.
Everything is made to order. The showroom in Karachi opened in June 2026.

The founding idea is simple: the people who make furniture in Pakistan have always sold
it through expensive retail chains that triple the price. Yousuf Living cuts that out.
You get the same quality, from the same craftsmen, at the price the workshop actually
charges.

**In one sentence:** Workshop Price. Showroom Quality.

**The customer** is a Karachi household — typically preparing a wedding (jahez/shaadi
bedroom set), setting up a new home, or upgrading a master bedroom. They care about
quality, they research on Instagram and TikTok before they buy, and they don't trust
brands that only show AI rooms with no real product behind them.

## 4. Visual Identity

### Colour palette

| Role | Name | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|---|
| Primary | Forest Green | `#1B4332` | Backgrounds, headers, CTA buttons, brand blocks |
| Accent | Warm Gold | `#C9A227` | Highlights, price reveals, key word boxes, leg details |
| Light | Cream | `#F5F0E8` | Light backgrounds, text on dark |
| Dark | Deep Charcoal | `#1A1A1A` | Body text on light backgrounds |
| Muted | Warm Grey | `#6B6B6B` (darkened from `#9E9E9E` — see note) | Secondary copy, captions |

**Never use:** pure white `#FFFFFF`, pure black `#000000`, or any cool-toned blue/grey.
The brand lives in warm, earthy, slightly moody tones.

> **Contrast note (checked with chroma-js, /brand-setup Stage 4):** the original
> Warm Grey `#9E9E9E` measures 2.36:1 on Cream — well under the 4.5:1 caption-legibility
> floor, so it's not safe for actual secondary/caption text. Darkened to `#6B6B6B`
> (4.70:1 on Cream) for any role where it's used as text. Gold `#C9A227` also fails as
> small foreground text directly on Cream (2.13:1) — but that's not how it's used here:
> per §4's own type rules, gold is a highlight *box* behind dark Charcoal text
> (7.19:1 — reads great), never small text-on-cream itself. Both checks pass at those
> actual usage patterns.

### The room palette (for AI room generation and real photography)
- Wall colour: warm cream or aged linen, never stark white
- Flooring: light oak herringbone parquet
- Lighting: 3500K warm white, single soft box from upper left, no harsh shadows
- Plants: 1–2 large leaf tropical (monstera or bird of paradise), deep green pots
- Props: gold vases, white/cream soft furnishings, red silk accents for bridal
- Window: sheer linen curtain, natural warm light bleeding in

For **bridal/shaadi variants** add: scattered rose petals (red), deep red silk throw,
tea lights on the dressing table.

### Typography

| Role | Font | Weight | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Display / headline | Instrument Serif | Regular, Italic | The brand's soul. Use for hero text, taglines, price reveals |
| Body / UI | Archivo | Regular, Medium, SemiBold | Clean, legible at small sizes |
| Supporting | Hanken Grotesk | Regular, Medium | Secondary copy, labels |
| Monospace / price | Space Mono | Regular | Prices, specs, tier labels |

**Type rules:**
- Headline in Instrument Serif, sentence case (not ALL CAPS)
- Key word emphasis: gold `#C9A227` highlight box behind the word, not underline
- Price always in Space Mono — it is a spec, not marketing copy
- Body copy line-height: 1.6 minimum. Never cramped.
- Roman Urdu (transliterated) is acceptable in captions but never in the visual
  design itself — keep the designed text in English

### Logo usage
- Primary: wordmark "Yousuf Living" in Instrument Serif, Forest Green on cream,
  or cream on Forest Green
- On dark backgrounds: cream wordmark
- On light backgrounds: forest green wordmark
- Gold accent: the dot above the "i" in "Living" (or the leg detail on icon mark)
- Minimum size: 80px wide in any rendered image
- Always in the bottom-right or bottom-left corner of a post — never centred top

### Photo and render style
- Always show the full piece or a deliberate detail — never a vague lifestyle shot
  where the furniture is background
- Gold legs must be visible in at least 50% of posts. They are the signature.
- Show the fabric texture up close at least once a week — it sells the quality
- Real showroom footage > AI room renders for trust. AI rooms for scale.
- No props that compete with the furniture. Simple, warm, deliberate.

## 5. Voice and Tone

### The brand voice in three words
**Direct. Warm. Confident.**

### What this sounds like
- Talks to you like a trusted friend who happens to make furniture
- Knows the product inside out and says so without being arrogant
- Mentions the real price upfront. No "DM for price."
- Acknowledges the Pakistani buyer's context: jahez pressure, budget planning,
  joint-family setups, Karachi traffic (delivery matters)

### What it never sounds like
- Corporate ("We are delighted to present...")
- Hype ("This is INSANE value!!!")
- Vague ("Quality you can trust." — says nothing)
- Pleading ("Please support us, small business")
- AI-written ("Elevate your space with our curated collection")

### Language rules
- **English primary.** Roman Urdu acceptable in captions for warmth and local context.
  Mix naturally — do not force it.
- **Sentence case always.** "Workshop price. Showroom quality." not "WORKSHOP PRICE."
- **Price is never hidden.** If the post is about a product, the price is in the
  caption. Either exact or "from Rs X." WhatsApp is for custom quotes only.
- **CTA is always WhatsApp or showroom.** Never "link in bio" — direct access only.
- **No hashtag spam.** Maximum 5–7 hashtags per post, relevant only. No #furniture
  #home #interior #decor #lifestyle #pakistan #karachi #love spray.

### Tone by platform
| Platform | Tone | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| TikTok | Casual, fast, hook-first | First 2 seconds must stop the scroll. Price reveal, quality proof, or a genuine question |
| Instagram Reels | Similar to TikTok but slightly more polished | |
| Instagram Feed | Warm and considered | The caption can be longer. Tell a small story |
| Facebook | Practical and direct | Mention delivery, showroom address, WhatsApp number |
| YouTube Shorts | Same as TikTok | |

## 11. Motion & Rendering (technical — for Remotion compositions)

This section is `/brand-setup`'s to maintain — it's the technical annex the business
sections above don't cover, needed so `packages/remotion/src/brand.ts` and
`fonts.ts` stay in sync with everything above. Business/voice/content decisions
still live in §1–§10; nothing here overrides them.

### Shape & depth
- **Radius:** ~16px cards/panels, pills fully rounded (matches the rounded, warm
  feel — no sharp corners, no hard offset shadows).
- **Depth:** soft shadows only (e.g. `0 8px 32px rgba(26,26,26,0.12)`), 1px warm
  hairline borders. No stark black shadows — keeps the "warm, earthy" rule.

### Motion language
Translated from §5's voice ("Direct. Warm. Confident.", premium-not-luxury, never
corporate or hype-loud):
- **Entrances:** fade + rise, ~14 frames, ease-out. Calm, no bounce/overshoot —
  same restrained shape as a premium brand, not a MrBeast-energy channel.
- **Emphasis:** gold highlight box behind a key word/price (§4's type rule), not an
  underline; scale pop kept subtle (max ~1.03).
- **Pacing:** short-form (TikTok/Reels/Shorts) means faster cuts and a stronger hook
  in the first 2 seconds than a long-form calm brand would use — confident and
  direct, not sluggish, but never gimmicky or bouncy.

### Video delivery specs
- **Canvas:** 1080×1920 (9:16), **30 fps** — the vertical short-form target for
  every platform this brand posts to (Facebook, Instagram, YouTube Shorts, TikTok).
  **Placeholder pending real camera confirmation**: if source clips are ever shot at
  a different frame rate, this must match the actual footage, not brand preference
  (a mismatch here desyncs visuals from narration silently — no error is raised).
- **Safe margins:** keep text ≥5% from edges. Gold legs and product texture (§4's
  "photo and render style" rules) must stay fully in frame — never crop the
  signature detail.
- **Captions:** overlays only, not word-level captions, consistent with the
  direct/warm caption voice in §5.

### Asset locations
- Brand tokens as code: `packages/remotion/src/brand.ts` · font families:
  `packages/remotion/src/fonts.ts`. **These two and this file are one contract in
  three places — keep them in sync** (`/brand-setup` writes all three at once).
- Logo: `media/library/logos/yousuf-living.svg`.
- Reusable brand assets generally: `media/library/` (typed folders — `logos/ sfx/
  music/` each with an index/catalog).
- Proof card: `cd packages/remotion && npx remotion still src/index.ts BrandProof
  out/brand-proof.png --frame=95`.
